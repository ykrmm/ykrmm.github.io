I am a postdoctoral NLP researcher between **INRIA Paris** and **INRIA Chile**, in the **ALMANACH** team. I work on **implicit reasoning** and how language models acquire knowledge during pretraining, how they compose it for multi-hop reasoning, and how to evaluate their socio-cultural biases, with a focus on Latin America.

I received my **PhD in Machine Learning** from [CNAM Paris](https://cedric.cnam.fr/) in June 2025, under the supervision of [Nicolas Thome](http://cedric.cnam.fr/~thomen/) and [Raphaël Fournier-S'niehotta](http://raphael.fournier-sniehotta.fr/) (CEDRIC, CNAM & ISIR, Sorbonne University). My thesis was on machine learning on graphs for recommendation and dynamic prediction.

My research covers:

- **LLMs & reasoning**: knowledge acquisition, multi-hop implicit knowledge composition, pretraining data augmentation ([Findings of EMNLP'26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09338)) ongoing work on Looped Transformers and Long-Context LMs.
- **Culture & bias**: socio-cultural bias evaluation of LLMs ([EACL'26 workshop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10001)), knowledge-graph augmentation vs. RAG for cultural QA ([ORACLE @ EMNLP'26](https://hal.science/hal-05870367))
- **Graph learning**: dynamic graph transformers ([NeurIPS'24](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.17986)), GNNs for recommendation ([TMLR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07912)), Graph-RAG ([ICMLC'26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05297))
- **Uncertainty**: failure prediction for vision-language models ([ICCV'25](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.07620))
